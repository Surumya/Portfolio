DreamStreet Capstone – Surumya Sharma
I created DreamStreet as a pivot from Dream11, with the goal of transforming India’s gaming audience into confident first‑time investors. The project focused on Tier 2/3 city users aged 21–40, where trust and confidence gaps remain the biggest barriers to stock market entry.

Research & Insights
I conducted a structured survey with 34 respondents to understand investment behaviour. The findings revealed that 56% feared market risk, 65% lacked time to research, and only 12% reacted positively to the Dream11‑to‑DreamStreet brand shift. SEBI registration emerged as the strongest trust driver (59%), while long KYC approval times were the top friction point (47%). These insights validated the confidence gap hypothesis and highlighted the need for education‑first onboarding.

Frameworks & Analysis
I applied TAM/SAM/SOM sizing, SWOT, competitive benchmarking, PESTEL, and a custom Trust‑Gap Analysis. This showed DreamStreet’s defensible moat: combining AI‑powered education (Veda) with SEBI‑certified advisors, a differentiator absent in Groww, Zerodha, or Jio BlackRock.

Solution Design
The MVP centred on two bets:

Rs 100 ETF starter path to reduce first‑investment paralysis.

KYC wait‑time engagement with lessons, WhatsApp nudges, and approval celebrations.

The experience blueprint mapped user flow from install to Day 90, with success metrics like 65% KYC completion, 40% first investment in 14 days, and 55% repeat investors.

Outcome
DreamStreet demonstrates end‑to‑end product thinking—from research and hypothesis validation to actionable strategy and prototype design—showcasing how trust, onboarding, and education can unlock India’s next wave of retail investors.
